'Test Long Mount' (Defs.cs, test-only), four FPV Teams one tap every
3 s of play from a pad 5,400 m out. Each sweep varies one of the mount's

  That row is the same 6,600 m mount as the top of (a), on the world every
  experiment ran on before AUDIT F34 was fixed. FINDINGS 33 recorded the
  two worlds as identical in every cell. They are not any more: the flat
  world has no territory owner, so every square metre of it is denied
  ground, every FPV Team's dead reckoning crosses the miss radius on the
  way in, and the warheads land on empty grass. Nothing about the mount
  changed. The world did, and something finally reads it.

  Read the two 'mount survives' columns against each other. They are the
  same mount, the same attack and the same seeds; the only difference is
  which of its two numbers is being spent. Whichever column moves is the
  one that is binding, and that is the number a player's upgrade should
  be buying.

  Both of them move, which is not what the old flat sweep implied and is
  not quite what FINDINGS 31 says either. The barrel matters a great
  deal here and mattered not at all before, and the difference is only
  the spacing: reach buys time, and time is worth nothing against a
  flight that arrives in one instant and everything against one that
  keeps arriving. 'Barrel length barely matters' is true of a wave and
  false of a stream.

  Both columns now run the same way, which they did not in compressed
  units: more barrel is better and more camera is better, and the two
  meet in the middle of the table. The old sweep read a *shorter*
  camera as the better buy - a belt spent at the rim of the envelope
  being a belt spent at the worst hit chance the falloff offers - and
  that inversion is gone. At real speeds an FPV Team covers the
  mount's kill ring in thirty real seconds rather than four, which is
  enough time for a second and a third engagement, so a mount that
  sees early can now afford to open early. The inversion was a
  property of a three-second engagement, not of the falloff.

  Read (a) and (b) against each other and the binding number is the
  seeing, not the barrel: the whole of (b) is fought with a 6,600 m
  barrel and still falls by thirty points, while the top half of (a)
  is fought with 7,200 m of camera and barely moves until the barrel
  drops below what the camera finds. That is FINDINGS 31 general
  claim surviving the rescale even though its arithmetic did not.
