
DECOY ESCORT - 2,600 Materiel of strike package against one turret
heavy strike drone 800, decoy drone 130
  world: mixed terrain (road, forest, rubble over open ground), weather Clear, ground Firm, territory split at 1550 m (team 1 west / team 2 east), imagery granted over each side's own ground

  The right-hand column is a control. It re-runs the same package with
  the decoy's radar cross-section cut to the strike drone's, leaving
  everything else alone - so the gap between the columns is what the
  reflector buys, as opposed to what simply being one more thing worth
  shooting at buys.

  package                 through (reflector)   through (no reflector)
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  3 real + 1 decoy                  3.00 of 3                3.00 of 3
  2 real + 6 decoy                  2.00 of 2                2.00 of 2
  1 real + 13 decoy                 1.00 of 1                1.00 of 1

  Nothing special-cases a decoy here. A defence picks targets by how
  much of one it can remove per shot, and a decoy dies to one shot
  just as a real drone does - so it is an equally good thing to shoot
  at, which is exactly the product being sold.

  The reflector, though, is doing nothing - the two columns agree to
  two decimals. It is not that the reflector is modelled wrongly: the
  radar finds a decoy at 2373 m and a strike drone at 750, which is
  the three-to-one the reporting describes. It is that the gun kills
  at 85 m and the battery at 320, so early warning arrives long
  before anything can act on it, and by the time something can, the
  cameras have the target anyway.

  A reflector decoy was never meant to fool gunnery. It is meant to
  make a defence spend an interceptor and a crew on an inflatable.
  Until a radar track can scramble something by itself, there is no
  decision here for a decoy to corrupt. See FINDINGS 25.
