
DECOY ESCORT - about 2,500 Materiel of strike package against one battery
heavy strike drone 800, decoy drone 130; the battery defends the radar
mast 1,200 m behind it, and the package is aimed at the mast (two warheads
on target destroy it)
arrivals spaced 2 s apart, so the battery's belt and its cooldown both bind
  world: mixed terrain (road, forest, rubble over open ground), weather Clear, ground Firm, territory split at 21600 m (team 1 west / team 2 east), imagery granted over each side's own ground

  The first row is the control: the same money, no decoys. The question
  is whether any row below it puts more warheads on the target than it
  does. There is no reflector column any more - see the comment on this
  method for why that control could not move and was not worth keeping.

  package                cost   warheads on target   mast killed   decoys shot   rounds
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  3 real + 0 decoy       2400            0.43 of 3           20%      0.0 of 0      6.1
  3 real + 1 decoy       2530            0.75 of 3           35%      0.8 of 1      7.3
  2 real + 6 decoy       2380            0.43 of 2            7%      5.6 of 6     21.2
  1 real + 13 decoy      2490            0.08 of 1            0%    13.0 of 13     41.5

  Nothing special-cases a decoy here. A defence picks targets by how
  much of one it can remove per shot, and a decoy dies to one round
  just as a strike drone does - so it is an equally good thing to shoot
  at, which is exactly the product being sold. The rounds column is
  whether the defence was made to spend it.

  The bottom row is the other end of the trade and it is not a failure
  of the mechanic: two warheads are needed to take the mast and that
  package only buys one, so it can saturate the battery completely and
  still not finish the job. Spending everything on escorts is the same
  mistake as spending nothing on them.
