  The left pair is not a tactic and should not be read as one. A Gun
  Mount cannot engage the high band at all, so a drone sent there is
  not evading the defence, it is outside it - which is why one high
  drone in the flight takes the mount's score to exactly zero and
  keeps it there. Its 100% column is a ceiling and carries no
  information; the quads-lost column beside it is the live one.

  The right pair is the question FINDINGS 18 was asking. The Autocannon
  reaches both bands, pays 20 ticks every time it re-lays across them,
  and loses 40% of its range and 30% of its hit chance shooting
  upward. Against it, splitting is worth something and committing
  everything high is worth slightly less than splitting - which is the
  shape FINDINGS 18 guessed at and could not measure, arrived at
  against a defence that is allowed to answer.
