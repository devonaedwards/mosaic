  A mount's reach is set by its gun and its envelope by whichever sensor
  finds the target first, and against a small electric quadcopter that
  is far shorter than the barrel can throw a round - which is why the
  survival columns move at all when the barrel never changes, and why
  they move so much less by day than after dark: in daylight every fit
  on this list finds the drone before the barrel can use the fact.

  Read the day and night reach columns together rather than separately.
  A camera beats a microphone against a quadcopter in daylight and
  collapses after dark; the microphone does not care what time it is
  and becomes the better of the two. Thermal buys back the night
  against a vehicle and very little against a small drone, because a
  small electric drone is not very hot.
