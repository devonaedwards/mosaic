
STACKING - more turrets covering the same approach
a cluster of mounts all covering one defended point, 450 Materiel each
  world: mixed terrain (road, forest, rubble over open ground), weather Clear, ground Firm, territory split at 1550 m (team 1 west / team 2 east), imagery granted over each side's own ground

  each cell: share of 40 trials in which the attack destroyed the lead
  mount. FPV Teams, 200 Materiel each, from a pad at 1,200 m. The last
  column is rounds fired per trial by each mount, lead first, averaged
  over every trial in the row.

  A mount is bought to service an engagement. A flight that arrives in
  one instant offers exactly one, and the rounds column says what that
  costs: against a wave the third and fourth mounts in a cluster fire
  nothing at all, because by the time a drone is 70 m from them it is
  already on top of the mount it was sent at. Against a stream the same
  four mounts all shoot, the lead one empties its five-round belt in
  nearly every trial, and the stack starts to be worth its money.
