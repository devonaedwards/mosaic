
MINES - a field laid across a supply road
four mines from one heavy drone, 600 damage each, into the underside
every number below is read off a vehicle driving through a live field,
not computed from the damage table; a + means the first mine killed it
outright, so the observed bite is a lower bound on the warhead
  world: mixed terrain (road, forest, rubble over open ground), weather Clear, ground Firm, territory split at 10800 m (team 2 west / team 1 east), imagery granted over each side's own ground

  vehicle             health off   survives a mine   field stops
  ------------------------------------------------------------
  Supply Truck            520+            0 of 4   yes, after 1 mine(s)
  Logistics UGV           500+            0 of 4   yes, after 1 mine(s)
  IFV                    1250+            0 of 4   yes, after 1 mine(s)
  Main Tank              1320             1 of 4   yes, after 2 mine(s)

  And the same field, driven into by the side that laid it:

  Supply Truck (own)      520+            0 of 4   yes, after 1 mine(s)

  A mine costs nothing to keep there. The heavy drone that laid it
  flew home and can do it again tomorrow night. It also does not ask
  whose vehicle arrives first, which is not a gameplay penalty - it is
  what a mine is, and a game about this subject should not pretend
  otherwise.
