  A gun mount takes three hits to destroy. 'arrived' is the average
  number of drones per attempt that lived long enough to strike it.

  Read the two per-kill columns against the two mounts' own prices,
  because surviving longer and being worth the money are different
  questions and this is the only table in the harness that separates
  them. The Autocannon absorbs three times the tap - six drones where
  the Gun Mount starts losing at three - and costs three and a half
  times as much. At its cheapest it makes an attacker spend about one
  and a half times its own price to remove it; the Gun Mount makes him
  spend more than twice. So the cheap mount is the better buy per
  Materiel and the expensive one is the better buy per position, and
  which of those a player wants is a map question rather than a
  balance one.
